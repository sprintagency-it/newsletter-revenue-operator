Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Newsletter Revenue Operator uses cookies, local storage and similar technologies on this website.

1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files stored by a browser. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and scripts that can store information, remember preferences or measure website activity.

2. Categories we use

Category Purpose Consent
Necessary Remember your cookie choice, keep the site usable and support security/hosting. Always active. These are needed for the site to work.
Marketing and analytics Load Meta Pixel and measure events such as PageView, Lead, InitiateCheckout and Purchase. Only active after you accept marketing cookies.

Name / provider Purpose Category Duration

Stripe Checkout Secure payment checkout when you choose the paid beta pack. Payment service Managed by Stripe when you visit or complete checkout.

4. Consent choices

On your first visit, the banner lets you accept all cookies, reject non-essential tracking or customize your choice. Marketing and analytics tracking is off by default and is loaded only after consent.

You can change your choice at any time by using the link in the footer.

5. Browser settings

You can also manage cookies and local storage from your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may affect parts of the website, form or checkout experience.

6. Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy when providers, tracking tools or the beta flow change.
